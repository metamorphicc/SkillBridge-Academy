# Brief

## Business

SkillBridge Academy is a fictional online school that sells a focused course or consultation program.

## Main Problem

Managers spend time on weak or incomplete applications. The business needs to quickly identify people who are ready to start and route them to the right next action.

## MVP Goal

Build a realistic lead qualification system that looks like a production automation case:

- public landing;
- Telegram qualification bot;
- lead score;
- CRM table;
- n8n workflow;
- manager alerts;
- follow-up reminders.
